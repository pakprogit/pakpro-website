Who we are
The Packaging Producer Responsibility Organisation (PAKPRO) is an award-winning, not-for-profit PRO incorporated in Kenya in 2018. It manages the collection, recovery and recycling of post-consumer packaging on behalf of its members.
Our mission
  • Inspire a shift toward responsible consumption and production.
  • Regulate post-consumer packaging waste.
  • Create shared value for stakeholders.
Why EPR matters
Under the Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Regulations, 2024, producers share responsibility for the packaging they place on the market. PAKPRO helps members meet that duty.
